## V1 Recap

GearVault V1 is a hard-shell, snap-lid pod designed to protect and organize loose backpack peripherals — earbuds, USB drives, cables, and dongles — that otherwise float around loose in a bag pocket. It ships in two size variants:

- **GearVault Mini** — smaller pod for compact items (earbuds, small dongles, short cables). 22g filament, ~2.5hr print time.
- **GearVault Pro** — larger pod for bulkier or more numerous items (multiple cables, larger dongles, USB drives). 38g filament, ~4hr print time.

Both variants use a snap-fit lid (no separate hinge or clasp hardware) and are produced via 3D printing (PLA), 5 units of each variant for the V1 batch (10 units total). Pricing follows a cost-plus model (see `07_Cost_Pricing/COST_PRICING_CALCULATION.md`).

*(Empty — fill in only after feedback themes above are populated. Use the prompts below as a guide for the kind of change to propose in response to real feedback.)*

- If feedback says the lid is hard to snap shut/open, consider: redesigning the snap-fit tolerance, adding a finger notch, or switching to a different closure mechanism.
- If feedback says the pod is too bulky/heavy for a backpack pocket, consider: thinning the shell walls, reducing overall dimensions, or adding an internal foam/lattice structure instead of solid walls.
- If feedback says items still rattle or shift inside, consider: adding internal dividers, foam padding, or shaped cutouts sized to specific items (earbuds case, USB drive slot, cable wrap post).
- If feedback says the color/finish looks unfinished or low-quality, consider: post-processing (sanding, painting), offering color options, or adjusting print settings (layer height, infill) for a smoother surface.
- If feedback says the Mini/Pro sizing doesn't match what people actually carry, consider: adjusting the two size tiers or adding a third size based on observed usage patterns.
