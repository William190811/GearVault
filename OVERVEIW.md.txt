MINI PRODUCT STARTUP

FIND A PROBLEM DESIGN MAKE SELL GET FEEDBACK IMPROVE

Create at least 10 units of your product. (5 unit per design)
Think like a manufacturer:
• How long does one unit take to print?
• How much material does it use?
• What happens if a print fails?
• Does it require assembly?
• How will you package it?
Don't start by asking “What should we make?”
Start by asking: “What problem can we solve?”

Design your solution using Fusion 360.
Requirements:
• Small & functional
• Maximum size: 80 × 80 × 80 mm
• 3D-printable
• Minimal support if possible
• At least 1 unique feature
• Consider production time and material usage
You are NOT limited to specific product examples. Be creative!
Identify a small, everyday problem experienced by your target customer.
• What is the problem?
• Who experiences it?
• How can your product solve it?

Collect feedback from at least 5 customers.
Google Forms • WhatsApp • Interview • Video • Voice recording • Written feedback • Rating form
Save the feedback and submit it with your project.
Your team must attempt to sell all 10 units.
You can sell to friends, teachers, school staff, family, or other people. You may use direct selling, WhatsApp,
Instagram, pre-order, a school booth, or other methods.
Not everything has to sell. A failed sale is still valuable data!
