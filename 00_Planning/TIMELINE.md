# Timeline — GearVault

Backward plan from the deadline of **2026-09-22**, starting today
**2026-09-08**. Two-week window, planned around a 2-3 person student team
working part-time alongside regular classes. Weekend days are used for the
heavier hands-on work (printing, batch assembly); weekday slots stay light.
