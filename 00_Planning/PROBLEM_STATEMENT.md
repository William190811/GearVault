# Problem Statement — GearVault

## The Problem

Loose peripherals carried in a backpack — earbuds, USB drives, small cables,
dongles — have no dedicated storage. They sit loose in the main compartment
or a shallow front pocket, where they:

- Get scratched, scuffed, or crushed by rubbing against textbooks, laptops,
  and other hard objects during normal daily movement.
- Get lost or buried at the bottom of the bag, wasting time digging for them
  between classes or meetings.
- Tangle with each other (cables around earbuds, earbuds around dongles),
  making them slower and more annoying to retrieve.

This is a small problem experienced daily, not an occasional inconvenience —
it repeats every time the bag is packed, opened, or shaken during transport.

## Who Experiences It

- **Students** — carry earbuds, a USB drive for assignments, a charging
  cable, and sometimes a dongle or SD card, all loose alongside textbooks
  and a laptop in the same backpack compartment.
- **Teachers** — carry a laptop plus a clicker, USB drive, or adapter between
  classrooms, with the same loose-item wear problem.
- **Office workers / commuters carrying laptops** — carry a laptop bag with
  a charging cable, mouse dongle, and earbuds that knock around against the
  laptop edge and screen during transit.

Common thread: anyone who packs a laptop or textbooks into the same
compartment as small loose electronics accessories.

## How GearVault Solves It

GearVault is a small hard-shell "vault" pod that lives inside the backpack
and gives these loose items one fixed, protected place:

- A hard-shell clamshell body absorbs the scratching and crushing contact
  that would otherwise land directly on the peripherals.
- Dedicated internal geometry (an earbud pocket, USB slots, and a center
  cable-wrap post) keeps each item in its own spot instead of loose and
  tangled, so items are protected AND findable by feel or by opening the lid.
- The snap-lid clamshell (print-in-place hinge, no hardware or glue) opens
  and closes in one motion, so it stays fast enough to actually use every
  day rather than becoming another zippered pouch people stop bothering with.

The goal is not a general organizer pouch — it is a small, purpose-fit,
durable pod sized to slide into an existing backpack pocket.
