# GearVault

GearVault is a compact 3D-printed backpack organizer designed to protect and organize small tech accessories such as earbud cases, USB drives, dongles, and charging cables.

The product was developed as a mini startup challenge project: identify a real daily problem, design a practical solution, manufacture units, gather customer feedback, and improve the design.

## Product at a Glance

- **Problem:** Loose accessories in bags get scratched, tangled, crushed, or lost.
- **Solution:** A small hard-shell storage pod with dedicated compartments.
- **Key feature:** Integrated center cable-wrap post to prevent cable tangling.
- **Closure system:** Friction-fit slip-on lid (simple, durable, no extra hardware).
- **Variants:** Mini and Pro versions, both within the 80 × 80 × 80 mm project limit.
